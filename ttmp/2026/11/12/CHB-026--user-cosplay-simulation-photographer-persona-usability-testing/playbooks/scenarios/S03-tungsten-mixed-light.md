# S03 Tungsten interior with mixed light

**In his words:** "Indoors under the lamps everything is orange. In the darkroom I'd dial in filtration; let's see
if that works here."

**Setup:** a fresh port. Files: 8538–8543 (six frames of the same interior, ISO 2000). Open as photos.

**What he does**
1. Opens all six. Picks the best one.
2. Removes the orange cast: first with whatever looks like white balance; then with filtration (C/M/Y) the way
   he would at the enlarger; tries the ring-around and test strip tools if he finds them.
3. Uses a densitometer or probe on something that should be neutral (a wall).
4. Makes a snapshot, tries a different paper or film, compares the two snapshots.
5. Applies the correction to the other five frames.

**Mistakes to make:** filtration in the wrong direction (more yellow instead of less); type `-5` into a
filtration box (is it relative or absolute?); type `30 CC`, `30cc`, `0,5`.

**Measure:** stalls on each filtration change; how long the ring-around or test strip takes to appear.

**Answer:** Did the darkroom metaphor help or hinder him? Could he tell which direction to move? Did the batch
apply work, and could he see that it did?
