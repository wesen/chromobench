# S08 Looks: films, papers, ring-around

**In his words:** "Make this one look like it was shot on Portra, and that one like old Agfa."

**Setup:** a fresh port. Files: 8559 (daylight), 8582 (daylight), 8655 (night). Open as photos.

**What he does**
1. Finds where films and papers are chosen (module tabs Print and Finish, the Looks variant via the "Layout"
   menu).
2. Tries five film stocks and three papers on 8559; keeps the one he likes as a snapshot; compares
   snapshots.
3. Uses the ring-around to choose a colour balance; uses the test strip to choose a time.
4. Saves the look and applies it to 8582.

**Mistakes to make:** pick a stock, undo, pick another quickly (four changes in two seconds); change the
layout in the middle of an edit (does the edit survive?).

**Measure:** time per stock change (tables may need building: first time vs repeat); ring-around build time.

**Answer:** Are the names of stocks and papers understandable? Is it fast enough to explore? Could he save and
re-use a look?
