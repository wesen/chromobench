# Reference

Every control in the Develop panel, in panel order, with:

- its id, the name used in recipes and in try-it links;
- its kind: macro, operator or hybrid (Chapter 1);
- the quantity it acts on;
- its range and default;
- its cost: free, rebuild or spatial;
- its formula, and the chapter that explains it.

The constants the formulas use are named as in the Studio's source and listed at the end.

The symbols:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $x_k$ | paper log exposure in layer $k$ |
| $g_k$ | the exposure that prints the aim grey |
| $PD_k$ | printing density |
| $\bar Y$ | the mean of the three printing densities |
| $B$ | the edge-aware base of $\bar Y$ |
| $c$ | the negative's dye amounts |
| $c_p$ | the paper's dye amounts |
| $L, C, h$ | OKLab lightness, chroma and hue |
| $e, w, t, \ldots$ | slider values |

A try-it link sets controls by id: `st:set?exposure=0.5&warmth=20`. The forms for the special types are:

- a pad: `shadowColour=30,-10`;
- a hue family: `hues.greens=10,-20`;
- a choice: `paper=kodak_supra_endura`.
